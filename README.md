# Top 10

Pick your ten favorite movies or TV shows and turn them into a shelf of VHS spines, tapes or covers.
Post to X makes one picture per spine.

Live site: https://lukbio.github.io/top10/

- Movie search uses Wikipedia. TV search, title logos and full-size posters use TMDB.
- Your lists are saved in your own browser only.
- `?list=tv` opens the TV list and `?view=tapes` opens the tapes view.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
